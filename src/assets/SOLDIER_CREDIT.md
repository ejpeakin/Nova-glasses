# Soldier character

`soldier.js` embeds the **SWAT operator character by Mixamo** (https://www.mixamo.com),
converted from Mixamo's FBX to glTF for the game (metres, indexed geometry, 1024 px
JPEG textures, animation clips removed).

Mixamo characters are royalty-free to use in personal and commercial games. They may
not be resold or redistributed as standalone character files; here the model is part
of the game. The game repaints it per faction at load time (camouflage uniform, kit
colour, balaclavas, the SWAT lettering painted out) and drives its skeleton from its
own procedural animation.
